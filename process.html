How We Work

A simple, transparent process designed to make your life easier

At Accountancy Chamber, we believe in making accounting simple and stress-free. Our proven process ensures you receive timely, accurate, and compliant accounting services while keeping you informed every step of the way. Here's how we work with you.

01

Initial Consultation

We start by understanding your business, your goals, and your specific accounting needs. During this free consultation, we discuss your current setup, challenges, and how we can help streamline your financial processes.

  • Free no-obligation discussion
  • Assessment of your current accounting setup
  • Understanding your business structure and needs
  • Identifying key priorities and deadlines
02

Tailored Service Plan

Based on our consultation, we create a customized service plan that matches your business requirements. We outline the services you need, agree on pricing, and set clear expectations for our working relationship.

  • Customized service package
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden fees
  • Clear service level agreements
  • Dedicated account manager assignment
03

Seamless Onboarding

We make the transition smooth and hassle-free. We'll set up your accounts, integrate with your preferred cloud accounting software (Xero, QuickBooks, or Sage), and establish secure document sharing systems.

  • Cloud accounting software setup
  • Secure document sharing portal access
  • Historical data migration if needed
  • Training and support on using our systems
04

Ongoing Support & Compliance

We handle your day-to-day accounting tasks, ensure all filings are submitted on time, and keep you compliant with HMRC regulations. You'll have regular access to your dedicated accountant for advice and support.

  • Regular bookkeeping and record maintenance
  • Timely VAT, payroll, and tax submissions
  • Proactive deadline management
  • Direct access to your accountant
05

Regular Reviews & Reporting

We provide regular financial reports and reviews to help you understand your business performance. Our insights help you make informed decisions and identify opportunities for growth and tax efficiency.

  • Monthly or quarterly management accounts
  • Performance analysis and insights
  • Cash flow monitoring
  • Profitability reviews
06

Year-End & Strategic Planning

At year-end, we prepare your annual accounts and tax returns with precision. We also provide strategic tax planning advice to minimize your tax liability and position your business for future success.

  • Annual accounts preparation
  • Corporation Tax and Self Assessment returns
  • Tax planning strategies
  • Business growth advice
