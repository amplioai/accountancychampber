How We Work

A clear process, from first call to year-round support.

Switching accountants — or appointing one for the first time — should feel easy. Our four-step process makes it simple, transparent and stress-free.

01

Discovery Call

We start with a free, no-obligation conversation. You tell us about your business, your current setup and what's working — or not. We listen, ask the right questions and identify exactly what you need.

Free · 30 Minutes
02

Fixed-Fee Proposal

Within 48 hours you receive a clear, written proposal. It sets out the services we'll provide, how we'll work together and one fixed fee — agreed upfront, with no hidden extras.

Within 48 Hours
03

Seamless Onboarding

We handle everything: contacting your previous accountant, transferring your records and setting up your cloud accounting software. You carry on running your business while we take care of the paperwork.

Week 1–2
04

Year-Round Compliance & Support

Your accounts, tax returns, payroll and VAT are prepared and filed on time, every time. And because we stay in touch all year, you get proactive advice — not just a once-a-year call.

Ongoing
