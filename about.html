About the Practice

Accountancy Chamber is the practice of Rafi Ullah Khan, MAAT — a Slough-based accountant providing personal, precise and proactive financial support to individuals and businesses across the UK.

The Person Behind the Practice

Rafi Ullah Khan, MAAT

As a Member of the Association of Accounting Technicians (MAAT), Rafi combines rigorous professional standards with a genuinely personal approach. Accountancy Chamber was founded on a simple belief: every business owner and individual deserves the same quality of accounting advice that large companies enjoy — without the complexity or the cost.

Based in Slough, Rafi works with sole traders, landlords, partnerships, small and medium-sized enterprises and limited companies across the UK. From day-to-day bookkeeping to year-end accounts and tax planning, clients get one consistent point of contact who knows their numbers inside out.
